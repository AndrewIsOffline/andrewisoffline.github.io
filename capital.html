Capitalism

Capitalism was an Android card video game based on a card game I played in high school (also called Capitalism, or commonly President). It's a fully featured app that even makes use of the Google Play Games API to update player achievements.

Due to software and hardware limitations at the time, I found it easier to write this app using fewer, larger files. The below code handled drawing and most non-gameplay functionality.
